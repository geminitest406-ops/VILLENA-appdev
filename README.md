# My Favorite Series🥳

## 🥇 1. Hunter x Hunter 

<img width="510" height="580" alt="Hunter x Hunter" src="https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/original/gOJt3Z00E0DTGE60ta1MH4XIhSK.jpg" />

*Hunter x Hunter follows the adventure of Gon Freecss, a young boy who dreams of becoming a professional Hunter to find his missing father, Ging Freecss. Along his journey, Gon meets Killua Zoldyck, Kurapika, and Leorio, who become his closest friends. Together, they face dangerous challenges, powerful enemies, and mysterious adventures. The series is known for its exciting battles, unique Nen abilities, emotional moments, and unforgettable friendships.*

**Hunter x Hunter Arc Guide**

**The Hunter x Hunter (2011) anime consists of 148 episodes divided into six main story arcs. Here's a brief guide to the arcs:**

* **Arc 1** Hunter Exam Arc - Introduces Gon Freecss and his friends as they participate in the dangerous Hunter Examination.

* **Arc 2** Heavens Arena Arc - Follows Gon and Killua as they train, compete in battles, and discover the mysterious power called Nen.

* **Arc 3** Yorknew City Arc - Focuses on Kurapika's revenge against the Phantom Troupe and the dangerous events in Yorknew City.

* **Arc 4** Greed Island Arc - Gon and Killua enter a mysterious video game to search for clues about Gon's father.

* **Arc 5** Chimera Ant Arc - Follows the Hunters as they face powerful Chimera Ants threatening humanity.

* **Arc 6** 13th Hunter Chairman Election Arc - Explores the election of a new Hunter Association chairman and Killua's efforts to save Gon.

## 🥈 2. Jujutsu Kaisen

<img width="510" height="580" alt="Jujutsu Kaisen" src="https://resemom.jp/imgs/ogp_f/303908.jpg" />

*Jujutsu Kaisen follows Yuji Itadori, a high school student who becomes involved in the dangerous world of curses after swallowing a powerful cursed object belonging to Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses. Yuji joins Tokyo Jujutsu High, where he trains under Satoru Gojo alongside Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki. Together, they fight deadly curses and powerful enemies while discovering the dark secrets of jujutsu sorcery. The series is known for its intense battles, supernatural abilities, emotional storytelling, and unforgettable characters.*

**Jujutsu Kaisen Season Guide**

**The Jujutsu Kaisen anime has three released seasons and a fourth season announced. Here's a brief guide to the seasons and their arcs:**

* **Season 1** Introduction, Cursed Womb, Vs. Mahito, Kyoto Goodwill Event, and Death Painting Arcs - Introduces Yuji Itadori, his classmates, and their battles against dangerous curses.

* **Movie** Jujutsu Kaisen 0 - Follows Yuta Okkotsu as he learns to control his cursed powers under the guidance of Satoru Gojo.

* **Season 2** Hidden Inventory / Premature Death Arc and Shibuya Incident Arc - Explores Gojo's past and the devastating battles that take place in Shibuya.

* **Season 3** The Culling Game Part 1 - Follows Yuji and his allies as they prepare for and enter a deadly competition involving powerful sorcerers.

* **Season 4** The Culling Game Part 2 - Announced continuation of the Culling Game storyline.

## 🥉 3. Naruto

<img width="510" height="580" alt="Naruto Shippuden" src="https://thetvdb.com/banners/posters/79824-15.jpg" />

*Naruto follows the journey of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who dreams of becoming Hokage, the strongest and most respected leader of his village. Despite being rejected by many villagers because of the Nine-Tailed Fox sealed inside him, Naruto never gives up on his dreams. Alongside his friends Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno, and his teacher Kakashi Hatake, he faces dangerous enemies, difficult missions, and emotional challenges. The series explores friendship, determination, sacrifice, and the importance of never giving up.*

**Naruto Series and Arc Guide**

**The Naruto anime is divided into two main series: Naruto, consisting of 220 episodes, and Naruto Shippuden, consisting of 500 episodes. Here's a brief guide to the major story arcs:**

**Naruto (Original Series)**

* **Arc 1** Land of Waves Arc - Introduces Team 7 as they complete a dangerous mission and encounter Zabuza and Haku.

* **Arc 2** Chunin Exams Arc - Naruto and his friends participate in the Chunin Exams, facing powerful opponents and difficult challenges.

* **Arc 3** Konoha Crush Arc - Follows Orochimaru's attack on the Hidden Leaf Village during the Chunin Exams.

* **Arc 4** Search for Tsunade Arc - Naruto travels with Jiraiya to find Tsunade and convince her to become the Fifth Hokage.

* **Arc 5** Sasuke Recovery Mission Arc - Naruto and his friends attempt to bring Sasuke back after he leaves the Hidden Leaf Village.

**Naruto Shippuden**

* **Arc 1** Kazekage Rescue Mission Arc - Naruto and his allies attempt to rescue Gaara from the Akatsuki organization.

* **Arc 2** Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission Arc - Team Kakashi searches for information about Sasuke and Orochimaru.

* **Arc 3** Akatsuki Suppression Mission Arc - Shikamaru and his allies confront powerful Akatsuki members.

* **Arc 4** Itachi Pursuit Mission Arc - Sasuke searches for his older brother Itachi while Naruto's team attempts to locate Sasuke.

* **Arc 5** Tale of Jiraiya the Gallant Arc - Jiraiya investigates the mysterious leader of the Akatsuki.

* **Arc 6** Pain's Assault Arc - Naruto faces Pain and fights to protect the Hidden Leaf Village.

* **Arc 7** Five Kage Summit Arc - The Five Kage gather to discuss the growing threat of the Akatsuki.

* **Arc 8** Fourth Shinobi World War Arc - The Allied Shinobi Forces unite against powerful enemies in a massive ninja war.

* **Arc 9** Kaguya Otsutsuki Strikes Arc - Naruto and Sasuke face an extremely powerful enemy while fighting to save the world.

* **Arc 10** Naruto and Sasuke Final Battle - Naruto and Sasuke confront each other in a final battle that decides their future.
